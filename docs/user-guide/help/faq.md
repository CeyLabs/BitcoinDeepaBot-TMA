# FAQ

## Getting started

### Do I need to download anything?

No. Bitcoin Deepa opens inside Telegram from [@BitcoinDeepaBot](https://t.me/BitcoinDeepaBot). You can also use it in a web browser and, if you like, [add it to your home screen](../installation/install-on-your-phone.md).

### Do I need a password?

No. You sign in with your Telegram account, both in Telegram and in a browser.

### Can I use it without verifying my identity?

You can open the app and look at the wallet, tasks and news. Subscribing to a plan, buying bitcoin, selling sats and adding a bank account all need a [verified identity](../setup/verify-your-identity.md).

### Why do you need my ID?

Buying and selling bitcoin for rupees involves payment and banking rules, so the service has to confirm who its members are. The verification screen confirms that your data is secured and never shared.

## Plans and buying

### What is the difference between a plan and a one-time buy?

A [plan](../setup/choose-a-plan.md) charges you automatically every week or month. A [one-time buy](../usage/buy-bitcoin.md) is a single purchase you make when you choose. You can use either, or both.

### How do I pay?

Through PayHere, in Sri Lankan rupees. The app sends you to PayHere's page to pay and brings you back afterwards.

### How much bitcoin will I get?

That depends on the bitcoin price at the moment your payment is confirmed. The app shows an estimate before you pay, and the exact amount and price afterwards in [Activity](../usage/activity.md).

### Are there fees?

Yes. The fee is always shown before you confirm a buy or a sale. For plans, your total fees are shown next to **You Invested** in the wallet.

### Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes, at any time, from **Plans → Manage Plan**. See [Manage your plan](../usage/manage-your-plan.md). Cancelling takes effect immediately.

### I cancelled my plan. What happens to my bitcoin?

It stays in your wallet. Cancelling only stops future payments.

## Selling

### How do I turn my bitcoin into rupees?

Use [Sell](../usage/sell-sats.md) on the Wallet tab. You need a verified [bank account](../setup/add-a-bank-account.md), and the rupees are sent there.

### Why do I have to upload a bank statement?

To confirm the account belongs to you. The name on the statement is checked against your verified identity before the first payout.

### What if the bank transfer fails?

Your sats are returned to your wallet automatically and the sale is marked **Refunded**.

## Wallet

### What are sats?

Sats, short for satoshis, are the smallest unit of bitcoin. One bitcoin is 100,000,000 sats. See the [Glossary](glossary.md).

### Why does my balance in rupees keep changing?

Your amount of bitcoin stays the same, but the price of bitcoin moves all the time, so its value in rupees moves with it.

### How do I hide my balance?

Tap the eye icon at the top of the Wallet, Activity or Plans tab. Tap it again to show the amounts.

### Can I send bitcoin to a friend?

Yes, to other Telegram users through the wallet bot. See [Wallet bot commands](../usage/bot-commands.md). Gifting a plan to a friend is marked **Coming Soon**.
