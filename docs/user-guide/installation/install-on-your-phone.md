# Install it on your phone

If you use Bitcoin Deepa in a web browser, you can add it to your home screen so it opens like a normal app. This is optional, and it is not needed inside Telegram.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open Bitcoin Deepa in Chrome and sign in.
2. On the **Wallet** tab, tap the **Install app** icon at the top right.
3. Confirm in the dialog that Chrome shows.

Bitcoin Deepa now has its own icon on your home screen.

## iPhone and iPad (Safari)

1. Open Bitcoin Deepa in Safari and sign in.
2. On the **Wallet** tab, tap the **Install app** icon at the top right. The app shows these same steps.
3. Tap the **Share** button in your browser.
4. Choose **Add to Home Screen**.
5. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner.

## Good to know

- The install icon only appears in a browser that supports installing, and disappears once the app is installed.
- The installed app updates itself. You never need to reinstall it to get a new version.
- If you lose your connection, the app shows a banner saying you are offline. You can still see the last data it loaded, but payments are paused until you are back online.
