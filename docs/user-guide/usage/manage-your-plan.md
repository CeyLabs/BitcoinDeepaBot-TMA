# Manage your plan

Once you have a plan, the **Plans** tab is where you check on it, switch to a different one, or cancel it.

Don't have a plan yet? See [Choose a plan](../setup/choose-a-plan.md).

## See how your plan is doing

Open the **Plans** tab. The **Manage My Plan** card shows:

- Your plan's name, price, and whether you pay per week or per month.
- An **Active** badge.
- **Subscribed Since** and **Next Reward** dates.
- **You Invested**: the rupees that went into bitcoin through this plan, the bitcoin you received, and any fees.
- **Current Value**: what that bitcoin is worth now, with your profit or loss.

Tap the eye icon to hide the amounts.

## Switch to a different plan

1. On the **Plans** tab, tap **Manage Plan**.
2. Your plan is shown under **Current Plan**. Below it, under **Choose a Different Plan**, pick **Weekly** or **Monthly** and tap the plan you want.
3. Tap **Continue with Selected Plan**.
4. Complete the payment on PayHere.

## Cancel your plan

1. On the **Plans** tab, tap **Manage Plan**.
2. Tap **Cancel Current Plan**.
3. Confirm with **Cancel Plan**, or tap **Keep Plan** to back out.

You see **Plan Cancelled** when it is done.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Cancelling takes effect immediately and cannot be undone: your plan stops buying bitcoin right away. The bitcoin already in your wallet stays yours. To start again later, choose a plan as you did the first time.
{% endhint %}
