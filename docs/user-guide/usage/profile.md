# Profile

Your profile holds your personal details, your verification status and your bank account.

To open it, tap your name or picture at the top left of the **Wallet** tab.

<!-- screenshot: the Profile screen -->

## Identity

Shows **KYC verification** with your current status: **Not started**, **In progress**, **In review**, **Verified**, **Declined**, **Expired** or **Not finished**.

Tap it to start, continue or check your verification. See [Verify your identity](../setup/verify-your-identity.md).

## Bank account

The account your rupees are paid into when you [sell sats](sell-sats.md), with its status: **In review**, **Verified** or **Rejected**.

- No account yet? Tap **Add bank account**.
- To change it, tap **Remove account**, then add the new one.

See [Add a bank account](../setup/add-a-bank-account.md) for the full steps. You need to be verified before you can add one.

## Personal details

Your **Name**, **Email**, **Phone** and **City**, as you entered them during verification.

If these are empty, tap **Complete your profile** to fill them in.

## Log out

In a web browser, **Log out** is at the bottom of the profile under **Account**.

Inside Telegram there is no log out, because you are signed in through your Telegram account. Close the app when you are done.
