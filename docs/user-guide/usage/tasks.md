# Tasks

The **Tasks** tab lists small things you can do to get more out of Bitcoin Deepa and help the community grow. Each task shows a reward in sats and whether it is a **Daily** or **One Time** task.

<!-- screenshot: the Tasks tab -->

## Filter the list

Use the tabs at the top: **All Tasks**, **Daily** or **One time**.

## The tasks

| Task                   | Type     | What happens when you tap it                                                                 |
| ---------------------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Setup your Wallet**  | One Time | Opens [@BitcoinDeepaBot](https://t.me/BitcoinDeepaBot), where you can receive and send sats. |
| **Join the Community** | One Time | Opens the Bitcoin Deepa community group on Telegram.                                         |
| **Share your Story**   | Daily    | Opens Telegram's story editor with a Bitcoin Deepa story and your personal invite link.      |

**Share your Story** only appears inside Telegram, because stories are a Telegram feature. In a web browser the task is hidden.

## Your invite link

The story you share carries an invite link that is unique to you. Friends who tap it open Bitcoin Deepa in Telegram.
