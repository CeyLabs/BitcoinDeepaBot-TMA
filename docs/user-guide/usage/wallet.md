# Wallet

The **Wallet** tab is the home screen. It shows how much bitcoin you hold, what it is worth, and how your plan is doing.

<!-- screenshot: the full Wallet tab -->

## Top bar

- **Your name and picture** (left): opens your [profile](profile.md).
- **Install app** icon: only in a web browser. See [Install it on your phone](../installation/install-on-your-phone.md).
- **Eye icon**: hides or shows every amount on the screen. Useful when someone is looking over your shoulder.

## Total Value

The large card shows your whole balance:

- The value in rupees, marked `≈` because it moves with the bitcoin price.
- The same balance in **BTC** and in **sats**.
- How much the value changed over the **Last 24h**, in rupees and as a percentage. Green means up, red means down.

Below the balance are two buttons: **[Buy](buy-bitcoin.md)** for a one-time purchase and **[Sell](sell-sats.md)** to turn sats into rupees.

If the card says **Balance unavailable**, the app could not reach your balance just now. Pull down to refresh.

## My Plan

Your current plan, its price, and an **Active** badge. Two smaller cards show:

- **Subscribed Since**: the date your plan started.
- **Next Reward**: the date of your next plan payment.

With no plan, this section says **No Active Plan**. Tap it to [choose one](../setup/choose-a-plan.md).

## Performance

Four cards summarise your plan's results:

| Card              | What it shows                                                                              |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **You Invested**  | The rupees that went into bitcoin, the bitcoin you received for them, and any fees on top. |
| **Current Value** | What that bitcoin is worth now, with your profit or loss in rupees and as a percentage.    |
| **Avg Price**     | The average bitcoin price you bought at, in LKR and USD.                                   |
| **Current Price** | The bitcoin price right now, in LKR and USD.                                               |

Your profit or loss is measured against everything you paid, fees included.

## Reward Portfolio Performance

A chart of your purchases over time. Tap or hover over it to see the values on a given date.

| Line          | Meaning                                        |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Portfolio** | The value of your bitcoin at today's price.    |
| **Invested**  | The total rupees that went into bitcoin.       |
| **Bitcoin**   | The bitcoin price at each purchase.            |
| **Avg BTC**   | Your average purchase price, as a dashed line. |

When **Portfolio** is above **Invested**, you are in profit. Before your first purchase, the chart is empty.

{% hint style="warning" %}
The bitcoin price goes down as well as up. The rupee values on this screen are estimates at the current price, not a guaranteed amount.
{% endhint %}
