# Buy bitcoin

A one-time buy lets you buy bitcoin whenever you like, with or without a plan. You pay in rupees through PayHere and the sats are added to your wallet.

{% hint style="info" %}
You need to [verify your identity](../setup/verify-your-identity.md) before you can buy.
{% endhint %}

## Steps

1. On the **Wallet** tab, tap **Buy**.
2. Enter the **Amount (LKR)**, or tap one of the quick amounts.
3. Check **You get (estimate)**, the fee and the rate.
4. Tap **Pay with PayHere** and complete the payment.

<!-- screenshot: the "Buy bitcoin" screen with an amount entered -->

## What the screen tells you

- **You get (estimate)**: roughly how many sats you will receive.
- **Fees**: the fee as a percentage and in rupees. It is taken from the amount you enter, so the rest goes into bitcoin.
- **1 BTC =**: the current bitcoin price in LKR.
- **Limits**: the smallest and largest amount for a single buy, and the most you can buy in a day.

The estimate uses the price at this moment. The final number of sats uses the price when your payment is confirmed, so it can be slightly higher or lower.

## After you pay

PayHere returns you to Bitcoin Deepa, which shows the status of your purchase:

| You see                           | What it means                                                                                              |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Waiting for PayHere**           | PayHere is confirming your payment. This usually takes a few seconds.                                      |
| **Payment received**              | You are paid up and your sats are being sent to your wallet.                                               |
| **Bitcoin received**              | Done. The sats are in your wallet.                                                                         |
| **Payment under review**          | Your payment arrived and the team is checking it before sending your sats. This usually takes a few hours. |
| **Payment Cancelled**             | You left the checkout without paying. You can try again any time.                                          |
| **Payment failed**                | The payment did not go through. Nothing was charged.                                                       |
| **No payment received**           | The checkout ran out of time with no payment.                                                              |
| **Still confirming your payment** | PayHere has not confirmed yet.                                                                             |
| **Payment reversed**              | Your bank reversed the payment. Contact support if you did not expect this.                                |

You do not have to keep the status page open. Once a payment is confirmed, the purchase appears in the **Activity** tab as a **Bitcoin Purchase**, and the sats show in your wallet.

{% hint style="warning" %}
If you paid and the status is still unclear, do not pay again. A confirmed payment always shows up in **Activity**. Check there first.
{% endhint %}

## Buy again

When a purchase is finished, tap **Buy again** to start another, or **Go to Dashboard** to return to your wallet.
