# Welcome to Bitcoin Deepa

Bitcoin Deepa (Bitcoin දීප) is a bitcoin membership app for Sri Lanka. You pay in rupees (LKR) and receive bitcoin, either automatically through a weekly or monthly plan or with a one-time buy. It runs inside Telegram as a Mini App, and also works in a normal web browser.

This guide covers everything from opening the app for the first time to selling your sats back to rupees.

## What you can do

- **Hold a bitcoin wallet** and see its value in LKR, BTC and sats.
- **Subscribe to a plan** that buys bitcoin for you every week or every month.
- **Buy bitcoin once**, whenever you like, and pay with PayHere.
- **Sell sats** and receive rupees in your Sri Lankan bank account.
- **Track every payment and transfer** in one activity feed.
- **Send and receive sats** with other Telegram users through the wallet bot.
- **Read bitcoin news** without leaving the app.

## What you need

- A Telegram account.
- A phone with a camera, to verify your identity.
- A National ID, passport or driving licence.
- A card or other payment method that PayHere accepts.
- A Sri Lankan bank account, if you want to sell sats for rupees.
