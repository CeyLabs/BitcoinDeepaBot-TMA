# Verify your identity

Before you can subscribe to a plan, buy bitcoin, sell sats or add a bank account, Bitcoin Deepa needs to confirm who you are. This is often called KYC ("know your customer"). You do it once.

## Before you start

Have these ready:

- **A phone with a camera.** Verification has to be done on a mobile device.
- **One identity document:** National ID, passport or driving licence.
- **Good lighting**, and nothing covering your face. Remove hats and sunglasses for the selfie.

Filling in the form and taking the photos takes about two minutes.

## Steps

1. Open the verification screen. Either:
   - tap **Complete Verification** on the **Verification Required** notice in the **Plans** tab, or
   - open your [profile](../usage/profile.md) and tap **KYC verification**.
2. Tap **Start Verification**.
3. Fill in **Your Details** and tap **Start Verification** again.
4. On the verification page that opens, photograph your identity document.
5. Take a selfie.
6. Wait for the review.

### Your details

| Field          | What to enter                                                 |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **First Name** | As it appears on your identity document.                      |
| **Last Name**  | As it appears on your identity document.                      |
| **Email**      | An address you check.                                         |
| **Phone**      | In international format, starting with `+94`: `+94771234567`. |
| **Address**    | Your street address, at least 5 characters.                   |
| **City**       | For example, Colombo.                                         |
| **Country**    | Filled in as Sri Lanka. Change it if needed.                  |

The **Start Verification** button stays disabled until every field is valid.

## While you wait

After you submit, the screen shows **Verification Under Review**. A review usually takes up to 24 hours, and you get a notification when it is approved. Tap **Back to Home** and keep using the wallet in the meantime.

You can check progress at any time under **KYC verification** in your profile.

| Status           | What it means                                        | What to do                                                   |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Not started**  | You have not begun verification.                     | Tap **Start Verification**.                                  |
| **In progress**  | You began but have not finished the photos.          | Tap **Continue Verification** to pick up where you left off. |
| **In review**    | Your documents were submitted and are being checked. | Wait. This usually takes up to 24 hours.                     |
| **Verified**     | You are approved.                                    | Tap **Continue to Plans**.                                   |
| **Declined**     | The documents could not be accepted.                 | Tap **Start New Verification**, or contact support.          |
| **Expired**      | The session, or an older approval, has run out.      | Tap **Start New Verification**.                              |
| **Not finished** | Verification was abandoned part-way.                 | Tap **Start New Verification**.                              |

## If you are on a computer

Tapping **Start Verification** on a desktop shows **Use Mobile Device**, because verification needs a phone camera. To continue:

1. Open Bitcoin Deepa on your phone, in Telegram or your mobile browser.
2. Sign in with the same Telegram account.
3. Start the verification from there.

## Next step

Once you are verified, the app takes you to [Choose a plan](choose-a-plan.md).
