# Choose a plan

A plan is a membership that charges you a fixed amount in rupees every week or every month and adds bitcoin to your wallet each time. You can cancel whenever you like.

{% hint style="info" %}
You need to [verify your identity](verify-your-identity.md) first. Until then, the **Plans** tab shows **Verification Required**.
{% endhint %}

A plan is optional. If you would rather buy now and then, skip this page and use a [one-time buy](../usage/buy-bitcoin.md).

## Steps

1. Open the **Plans** tab. With no active plan, it opens **Choose Your Plan**.
2. Pick **Weekly** or **Monthly** with the switch at the top.
3. Tap a plan to select it. The card expands to show what you would pay **Per Month** and **Per Year**.
4. Tap **Continue with Selected Plan**.
5. Pay on the PayHere page that opens.

<!-- screenshot: "Choose Your Plan" with one plan selected and expanded -->

Each plan shows its name, its price in LKR, and whether the price is per week or per month. One plan may carry a **Most Popular** badge.

Not ready yet? Tap **Skip to Wallet** at the bottom.

## After you pay

| You see                | What happened                                                                          |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Payment Successful** | Your plan is active. Tap **Go to Dashboard**.                                          |
| **Payment Cancelled**  | The payment was not completed and nothing changed. Tap **Back to Plans** to try again. |

Once your plan is active:

- The **Wallet** tab shows it under **My Plan** with a green **Active** badge, the date you subscribed, and the date of your **Next Reward**.
- Each payment appears in the **Activity** tab as a **Membership Reward**.

## What it costs

The price on the plan card is what you are charged each period. Any fees taken from a payment are shown in your wallet next to **You Invested**, so you can always see how much went into bitcoin and how much went to fees.
